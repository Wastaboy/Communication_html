What Part 5 builds

TL;DRTwo Power Automate flows — CBF - New Request Notification (email the CCD team on every submission, now carrying division, launch date and deliverables) and CBF - Audit Logger (a reportable row in Audit Log for every create, edit and status change) — plus two optional flows (status-change email to the requester, and an assignment email to the CCD member a request is assigned to), test-data cleanup, and the full acceptance walkthrough that takes the system live.
Changed since the last guide (17 Aug edition)One expression changed: the deliverables column's internal name in the live list is TypeofDeliverables — lowercase o — and e2's Select expression now spells it that way. Flow expressions address columns by their internal name, which froze at creation and is case-sensitive: body/TypeOfDeliverables silently returns nothing on this list, while the ⚡ dynamic chips always show the friendly display labels ('Division', 'CCM Notes') and are unaffected by any of this. If an expression in this part ever returns blank against a healthy item, check its internal-name spelling first.

Why the Select + join danceA multi-select choice arrives as a list of records, not text — dropped straight into an email it renders as raw JSON. The Select action strips each record down to its value; join glues them with commas: Event Support, Media & PR. The underscores in body('Select_-_Deliverables') are how expressions spell the card name's spaces.
