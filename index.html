Communication Briefing Form

Five guided parts that take a blank SharePoint tenant to a working briefing-request system — form, tracking, audit trail, notifications.

5 parts ~3.5 hours total SharePoint · Power Apps · Power Automate

What this series builds

The systemStaff submit communication briefing requests through a form as simple as a Google Form — with attachments. The CCD team gets an email on every submission, tracks and updates each request on a dashboard, and every edit lands in an audit trail with name and timestamp. Requesters see their own requests (and only their own), live status included. Everything exports to Excel.
Phase-1 ruleBy explicit business decision there is no approval or rejection workflow anywhere in this build. Keeping the requester experience simple is the core principle every part protects.

Before Part 1

You will need

  • A BBK Microsoft 365 account that can create SharePoint team sites, canvas apps and flows (or IT on call for the site).
  • The five CCD member names — and, by Part 4, their real email addresses.
  • Roughly 3.5 focused hours across the five parts. Each part ends with a working, tested layer — safe places to pause.

The five parts

Part 1 · SharePoint · 9 steps · ~40 min

SharePoint Foundation — the private team site, the Communication Requests and Audit Log lists, versioning, attachments, item-level privacy, and CCD access. The data layer, finished and verified.

Part 2 · Power Apps · 10 steps · ~45 min

App Shell + Submission Form — the Communication Briefing Form canvas app and its Google-Form-simple submit screen: five fields, validation, attachment upload, and a real end-to-end submit test.

Part 3 · Power Apps · 10 steps · ~45 min

My Requests — requesters see their own submissions with live status colors and CCD notes, and reopen any request to edit it. The shared form learns Edit mode.

Part 4 · Power Apps · 10 steps · ~45 min

CCD Dashboard — the team-only screen: every request, status filter chips, search, a status + notes update panel requesters see instantly, and the native Excel export.

Part 5 · Power Automate · 12 steps · ~50 min

Flows + Go-Live — the CCD notification email, the audit-logger flow (created / edited / status changed), an optional requester notification, test-data cleanup, and the full ten-point acceptance walkthrough.

How the guides work

  • Amber chips are exact names and values — tap one to copy it.
  • Navy buttons are things you click in SharePoint, Power Apps or Power Automate.
  • Dark boxes hold formulas with their own Copy button — paste them verbatim.
  • Every step ends with a green check; every part ends with an F5 test. Progress is saved in your browser per page.
